Template revision example: Ahmad Fakhry

The sample in the school template contains several errors that commonly occur when a narrative is copied from another student. Highlighted text needs revision.

  1. Inconsistent name. The name appears as AHMAD, Akhmad and Akhmat, with a leftover placeholder Fulanah. Use one spelling that matches the student record.
  2. Mixed pronouns. The Social narrative uses he/his, while the Spiritual narrative uses she/her. A male student requires he/his/him throughout.
  3. Spelling and grammar. Intellegence → Intelligence; almost completing in assignments → completed almost all assignments; due she needs → as he needs.
  4. Grades not explained. Independence is graded Need Improvement, yet none of its sub-values (Responsibility, Risk-Taking, Self-confidence, Resilience, Self-Control) is discussed or followed by a recommendation. Self-control is mentioned only in the Spiritual paragraph.
  5. Sub-values not named. Linking statements to the sub-values (e.g. Problem-solving, Creative-Innovation, Care) shows parents exactly which part of the framework is being assessed.
  6. No evidence. No specific incident is described. One example from an anecdotal record makes the narrative more credible to parents.
  7. Vague recommendation. Activities that promote imaginative thinking should name activities that can be carried out immediately.
  8. Inconsistent timing. The report is dated 1 September, at the start of Semester 1, yet refers to throughout the academic year. Use this term instead.
Before · Social

Akhmad Fakhry has been demonstrating responsibility throughout the academic year. He has been almost completing in assignments on time and has shown a keen awareness of his academic obligations. While Akhmad Fakhry has been a diligent student, he has been exhibiting a tendency to rely on conventional approaches to problem-solving. His creative thinking skills could be further developed. Therefore, he is encouraged to participate in activities that promote imaginative thinking.

After · Social

Ahmad Fakhry has been demonstrating responsibility this term; he completed almost all assignments on time and is aware of his academic obligations. However, he still relies on familiar approaches to problem-solving, and his creative-innovative thinking can be developed further. He also needs frequent reminders to stay focused, which indicates that his self-control is still developing. Therefore, he is encouraged to take part in open-ended projects and brainstorming activities, and to use an agreed signal before speaking so that he can manage his impulses independently.

Before · Spiritual

Masyaallah, Akhmat has continued to demonstrate her empathy. She manages her emotions appropriately and positively responds to her friends during free play. However, Fulanah is still developing her self-control due she needs to be reminded about staying focused and avoiding distraction. Therefore, raising her hands and focusing on class will improve her self-control.

After · Spiritual

Masyaallah, Ahmad has continued to show genuine care for the feelings of his friends and responds to them courteously during free play. He performs his daily prayers consistently and expresses gratitude for the blessings he receives. To grow further, he is encouraged to lead the du'a in class occasionally so that he can become a role model in worship for his peers.
